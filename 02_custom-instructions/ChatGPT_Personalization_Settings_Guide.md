### **10. Saved Memories: `ON` (Change from OFF)**

**Current:** OFF  
**Recommended:** ON

**What it does:**
- ChatGPT saves key facts about you across conversations
- You can tell ChatGPT "remember this" and it persists
- Helps with continuity without re-explaining everything

**Why turn it ON:**
- ✅ You build repeatable systems (Smartsheet, Excel templates)
- ✅ ChatGPT needs to remember your context
- ✅ Reduces need to re-explain your domains every conversation
- ✅ Supports continuity across projects

**Example use:**
- You tell ChatGPT: "Remember: I use Smartsheet for scheduling and OrganOx for perfusion"
- ChatGPT recalls this in future conversations

**Action:** Turn ON in Memory section

### **11. Chat History Reference: `ON` (Change from OFF)**

**Current:** OFF  
**Recommended:** ON

**What it does:**
- ChatGPT can reference recent conversations in the same chat
- Helps with multi-turn complex projects
- Reduces context loss mid-project

**Why turn it ON:**
- ✅ You work on complex multi-turn builds (Excel templates, Smartsheet workflows)
- ✅ ChatGPT won't lose context as conversation gets long
- ✅ You can reference "like we did earlier" without explaining again
- ✅ Essential for large projects that span multiple messages

**How it works:**
- ChatGPT automatically tracks conversation history
- You can ask: "Remember when we added the formula earlier?"
- ChatGPT references that earlier work

**Action:** Turn ON in Memory section
